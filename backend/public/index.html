Ledger

HR & Payroll Management System

Built for G-Tech Africa Ltd — payroll processing, statutory compliance, and HR administration, all in one place.

Our Mission

To give Kenyan businesses of every size the tools to run payroll and HR with the accuracy, compliance, and ease that used to be reserved for large enterprises — replacing spreadsheets and guesswork with a system that gets PAYE, NSSF, SHA, and the Housing Levy right, every time.

Our Vision

A Kenya where no business owner loses a night's sleep over payroll — where compliance is automatic, HR is organized, and every employee is paid correctly and on time, whether the company has five people or five hundred.

What We're Targeting

Kenyan SMEs and NGOs, especially those still running payroll in Excel or by hand — and industries where overtime, shift work, and safety compliance genuinely matter: retail, hospitality, construction, manufacturing, logistics, and security.

◆

Payroll, done right

PAYE, NSSF, SHA and the Housing Levy computed automatically on every run. Downloadable payslips and KRA-style P9 forms, generated straight from your records.

◆

Full HR toolkit

Employee records with departments and documents, leave requests and approvals, daily attendance, and performance reviews.

◆

Access that makes sense

Admin, Staff, and HR-only roles. Salary and bank details stay restricted to those who should see them.
